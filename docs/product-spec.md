# Product Specification: AXIOM

Autonomous Operations. Autonomous intelligence, under human control.

## Executive Summary

AXIOM delivers autonomous multi-agent orchestration for enterprise workflows while maintaining strict human-in-the-loop governance.

## System Capabilities

- Multi-Agent Orchestration: 8 active agents collaborating across defined domains.
- Policy Gating: Automatic circuit breakers for financial, infrastructure, and privacy actions.
- Transparent Auditing: Tamper-evident cryptographic ledger for all actions.
- Seamless Navigation: Dual-layer experience with an editorial front door and an operations console.
